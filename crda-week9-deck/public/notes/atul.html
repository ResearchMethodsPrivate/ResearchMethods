Words in plain English

Plain model
The same model without CRDA.
MLP / XGBoost
Two kinds of prediction model: a small neural network, and a set of decision trees.
Error (MSE)
The average size of the model’s mistakes on data it has not seen. Lower is better.
Safety test
CRDA’s own check. It is meant to switch CRDA off when it does not clearly help.
10-model average
Train 10 models on slightly different slices of the training data and average their predictions. Averaging usually lowers error by itself.
Problem log
Our shared list of every difference and problem we found: 17 entries.

Likely questions

Why compare the plain models first?

If our plain models differed from the authors’, we could not trust any comparison of the gains.

Why does plain XGBoost differ on Windows?

We do not know yet. Two different Windows laptops give the same number, so it is not random noise. The Mac matches the paper.

Why does the gain differ between laptops?

A single repeat’s gain swings between about −67% and +25%, so a few points between laptops is expected.

What are the two catches?

One: the paper’s table keeps runs its own safety test would reject. Two: CRDA’s score averages 10 models but the plain score uses one, and much of the gain is just that averaging. For XGBoost it is most or all of it.

What is in the problem log?

Setup problems (all resolved), the Windows difference, the two catches, and how some numbers in the files are defined. 17 entries.

What happens next?

We decide how to report CRDA’s gain in the final. Samim and I write up the statistics and check every number. All four make the final slides and rehearse.

Where can people check your numbers?

Everything is in our group repository, in the results folder.

Don't say

  • That we know why Windows differs. Say “we do not know yet”.
  • That the paper is wrong. Say “its table is not like-for-like”.
  • New numbers that are not on the slides.
